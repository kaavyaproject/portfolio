Featured Projects

Toy Design

Participated in the Cognify toy design competition as part of a team. Designed a toy for neurodivergent children to train saccadic eye movements and created its 3D model using Tinkercad.

Oscilipillar is an interactive, modular toy designed for children aged 3 to 5 years. It features oscillating, glitter-filled bobs that move like pendulums, capturing visual attention and encouraging coordinated eye movements. The toy includes a tactile lock mechanism, a foam-covered top for sensory engagement, and gentle sound feedback to reinforce interaction. Its front panel can be changed as the child grows, allowing themes aligned with evolving interests. Oscilipillar is inclusive and suitable for both neurotypical and neurodivergent children, with the goal of improving attentional control through coordinated saccadic movements.

Design Thinking Neurodiversity Inclusive Design
